Questions

Do listeners need an account?

No. Anyone with the link can listen in the browser, or follow the show's podcast feed. Only the people who upload sign in.

What can I upload?

MP3, M4A/AAC, OGG and WAV files up to 100 MB each, plus a square cover image. Earshot checks the file before it uploads anything.

How long are recordings kept?

Each upload is stored on Walrus for a set number of storage epochs, chosen by whoever runs the Earshot server. The admin page shows when storage ends.

Can I take an episode down?

Yes. Hiding an episode removes it from your show page and feed straight away. The file stays on Walrus until its storage period ends, or until it is deleted.

Does it work with Apple Podcasts and Spotify?

Apple Podcasts, Pocket Casts, Overcast, AntennaPod and most other apps can follow a show by its RSS link. Spotify needs its own submission.

Is Earshot open source?

Yes. It is MIT licensed and built with Dartus, the Dart SDK for Walrus.
